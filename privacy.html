ClarkVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

🔐 Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains what information ClarkVerse may collect when you use the platform, how that information may be used, and the choices you may have regarding your information.

By using ClarkVerse, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Information You Provide

When you create and use a ClarkVerse account, information you provide may include:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials
  • Username
  • Profile information
  • Comments
  • Verses and other uploaded content
  • Information you provide when contacting support

2. Camera and Microphone

ClarkVerse may request access to your device's camera and microphone when you choose to create a Verse.

Camera and microphone access is requested through your device or browser permission system.

You can deny these permissions, although certain creation features may not work without them.

3. Photos and Videos

If you choose to upload a video or other media, ClarkVerse may receive the media you select for uploading.

ClarkVerse does not need access to your entire photo library simply because you have an account. Media access is requested when you use features that require it.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information associated with your account may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide ClarkVerse features
  • Display your profile and content
  • Store comments and uploaded content
  • Provide customer support
  • Help maintain platform security
  • Detect and respond to rule violations
  • Improve the platform

5. Content You Post

Content you intentionally post to ClarkVerse, such as Verses, comments, usernames, and profile information, may be visible to other users depending on the features of the platform.

Do not post private information that you do not want other people to see.

6. Moderation

ClarkVerse may process uploaded content, usernames, comments, and other platform activity to help enforce the ClarkVerse Rules.

Moderation may involve automated systems and, where appropriate, human review.

7. Information Security

ClarkVerse uses reasonable measures intended to protect information associated with the platform.

However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

8. Service Providers

ClarkVerse may use third-party services to help operate features such as authentication, hosting, storage, databases, analytics, or security.

These services may process information as needed to provide their functions.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ClarkVerse or services used by ClarkVerse may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support functionality, preferences, security, and other platform features.

10. Links to Other Services

ClarkVerse may contain links or integrations to other websites or services.

ClarkVerse is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services. Review their privacy policies before using them.

11. Children's Privacy

ClarkVerse is not intended for people who are not legally permitted to use the service under the applicable age requirements.

If you believe a child has provided information to ClarkVerse in violation of the applicable requirements, contact support.

12. Account Information

You may be able to update certain account information through the features provided by ClarkVerse.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

13. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate ClarkVerse, provide services, enforce rules, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with applicable requirements.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ClarkVerse develops or as privacy requirements change.

If important changes are made, ClarkVerse may provide notice through the platform or other appropriate methods.

15. Your Agreement

By continuing through the ClarkVerse onboarding process, you acknowledge that you have reviewed this Privacy Policy.

You will also need to review the ClarkVerse Rules before entering the Ship.
