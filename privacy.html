ClarkVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ClarkVerse may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the ClarkVerse website or application.

By using ClarkVerse, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, ClarkVerse may collect information that you choose to provide, including:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Videos and other content you upload
  • Comments and other content you post
  • Information you provide when contacting support

3. Camera and Microphone

ClarkVerse may request access to your device's camera and microphone when you choose to record a Verse.

Camera and microphone access is requested by your device or browser. You can deny these permissions, although some recording features may not work without them.

4. Photo and Video Library

When you choose to upload a video, ClarkVerse may request access to your device's photo or video library.

This permission allows you to select media that you choose to upload as a Verse.

ClarkVerse does not need access to your entire photo library simply for you to browse the Ship.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected by ClarkVerse may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide the ClarkVerse Ship and other features
  • Store and display content you choose to upload
  • Allow users to comment on and like content
  • Provide moderation and safety features
  • Respond to support requests
  • Detect spam, abuse, and attempts to harm the platform
  • Improve ClarkVerse
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access

6. User Content

Content you voluntarily upload or post to ClarkVerse may be stored and displayed to other users according to the features you use and your account settings.

Do not upload information that you do not want other people to see.

7. Comments and Likes

Comments, likes, and similar interactions may be associated with your account and stored so that they remain available when you return to ClarkVerse.

8. Moderation and Safety

ClarkVerse may use automated systems and, where appropriate, human review to help detect violations of the ClarkVerse Terms & Conditions and Rules.

This may involve analyzing content such as usernames, comments, and uploaded videos for safety and moderation purposes.

9. Account Information

ClarkVerse may use an authentication service to securely manage account sign-in and account-related information.

Your authentication information may be processed and stored by the services used to operate ClarkVerse.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

ClarkVerse may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, maintain sessions, and provide certain features.

11. Third-Party Services

ClarkVerse may use third-party services to operate parts of the platform, such as authentication, databases, file storage, hosting, security, and other technical infrastructure.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

12. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information used by ClarkVerse.

However, no internet service can guarantee that information will always be completely secure.

13. Information Sharing

ClarkVerse does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate ClarkVerse, provide requested features, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

14. Public Information

Some information you choose to publish on ClarkVerse may be visible to other users or the public.

This may include your username, profile information, uploaded Verses, comments, likes, and other information you intentionally make available through the platform.

15. Children's Privacy

ClarkVerse is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that violates applicable law, contact ClarkVerse support so the situation can be reviewed.

16. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide ClarkVerse, maintain accounts, enforce rules, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

17. Deleting Your Account

ClarkVerse may provide account deletion or data-management options as the platform develops.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

If significant changes are made, ClarkVerse may require users to review and accept the updated policy before continuing to use certain features.

19. Your Agreement

By using ClarkVerse, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how information may be handled when using the platform.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the ClarkVerse Terms & Conditions and ClarkVerse Rules.
