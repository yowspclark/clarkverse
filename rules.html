ClarkVerse Rules

Last updated: August 2026

🚢 Welcome Aboard

These are the rules every ClarkVerse user must follow.

The goal is simple: make ClarkVerse a fun, creative, and safe place for everyone.

1 Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect.

  • No serious harassment.
  • No targeted bullying.
  • No intimidation or repeated abuse.
  • Do not intentionally ruin another user's experience.

2 No Doxxing

Doxxing is strictly prohibited.

  • Do not post someone's home address.
  • Do not post someone's private phone number.
  • Do not expose private email addresses.
  • Do not share private account information.
  • Do not encourage others to find someone's private information.

Serious doxxing can result in immediate account termination.

3 No Threats

Do not make credible threats against other users or people.

  • No threats of violence.
  • No threats intended to intimidate users.
  • No encouraging others to harm someone.

4 No Hate-Based Harassment

Do not target people with harassment, slurs, or hateful attacks based on protected characteristics.

5 No Sexual Content

Do not upload or distribute prohibited sexual content.

Sexual content involving minors is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate termination and appropriate reporting.

6 Keep Usernames Appropriate

Usernames must be appropriate for the ClarkVerse community.

  • No hateful usernames.
  • No threatening usernames.
  • No explicit usernames.
  • No usernames impersonating staff or other users.
  • No usernames designed to bypass moderation.

7 No Scams or Fraud

Do not use ClarkVerse to scam, deceive, or defraud other users.

  • No fake giveaways intended to steal information.
  • No phishing.
  • No payment scams.
  • No impersonation for financial gain.

8 No Malicious Software

Do not distribute malware, viruses, malicious scripts, or other software designed to harm devices or accounts.

9 Respect Copyright

Only upload content that you have the right or permission to share.

Do not intentionally upload copyrighted material in a way that violates the rights of its owner.

10 Don't Evade Moderation

If your account or content is restricted, do not attempt to bypass the restriction by repeatedly creating accounts or using other methods to evade enforcement.

11 Don't Abuse the Platform

  • No spam.
  • No automated abuse of ClarkVerse features.
  • No attempts to disrupt the service.
  • No attempts to exploit security vulnerabilities.

12 Follow the Law

Do not use ClarkVerse for illegal activities or to facilitate illegal activity.

🛡️ Moderation Actions

ClarkVerse uses simple moderation systems to help identify inappropriate usernames, comments, and content.

Depending on the violation, moderation may result in:

  • Content removal
  • A warning
  • A temporary ban
  • Feature restrictions
  • Account termination

Serious violations may result in immediate termination.
