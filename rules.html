ClarkVerse Rules

Last updated: August 2026

🚢 Welcome Aboard the Ship

ClarkVerse is a place to create, share, and discover Verses. These rules help keep the Ship safe and enjoyable for everyone.

By using ClarkVerse, you agree to follow these rules along with the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect.

  • Do not harass other users.
  • Do not repeatedly target someone with abusive behavior.
  • Do not threaten other users.
  • Do not encourage people to harm themselves or others.
  • Do not intentionally bully or intimidate people.

2. 🚨 No Doxxing

Doxxing is prohibited.

Do not post or distribute someone's private personal information without their permission.

  • Home addresses
  • Private phone numbers
  • Private email addresses
  • Passwords or login information
  • Private financial information
  • Private location information
  • Other sensitive personal information

Serious doxxing may result in immediate account termination.

3. No Threats

Do not threaten violence or encourage violence against another person or group.

Serious threats may result in immediate moderation action, including account termination.

4. No Hate or Targeted Abuse

Do not attack or target people based on protected characteristics or use hateful slurs or hateful harassment.

Criticism and disagreements are allowed, but targeted abuse and hateful conduct are not.

5. No Sexual or Exploitative Content

Do not use ClarkVerse to distribute prohibited sexual or exploitative content.

Any content involving the sexual exploitation of minors is strictly prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities where required.

6. No Graphic or Extremely Violent Content

Do not upload extremely graphic violence, gore, or content intended primarily to shock or encourage violence.

7. Keep Usernames Appropriate

Usernames must be appropriate for ClarkVerse.

  • No hateful usernames.
  • No threatening usernames.
  • No sexually explicit usernames.
  • No usernames containing private personal information.
  • No impersonation of other people or organizations.
  • No usernames designed to evade moderation.

Inappropriate usernames may be changed, restricted, or removed.

8. No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be another user, creator, public figure, organization, or ClarkVerse staff member in order to deceive people.

9. No Spam

Do not flood ClarkVerse with unwanted or repetitive content.

  • No repeated comments intended to disrupt conversations.
  • No automated spam.
  • No fake engagement.
  • No mass-following or similar abusive behavior.
  • No attempts to manipulate likes or views.

10. No Scams or Fraud

Do not use ClarkVerse to scam, defraud, deceive, or steal from other users.

Do not impersonate ClarkVerse staff to obtain passwords, authentication codes, money, or other sensitive information.

11. No Malicious Activity

Do not attempt to damage, disrupt, exploit, or gain unauthorized access to ClarkVerse or another user's account.

  • No hacking attempts.
  • No credential theft.
  • No malware distribution.
  • No attempts to bypass security.
  • No attempts to bypass moderation systems.

12. Upload Content You Have the Right to Share

Only upload videos, images, music, or other content that you have permission to use.

Do not knowingly upload copyrighted material that you do not have the rights or permission to distribute.

13. Don't Abuse Reports

Reports should be used to notify ClarkVerse about genuine concerns.

Do not intentionally submit false reports to harass another user or abuse the moderation system.

14. Don't Evade a Ban

If your account is temporarily suspended or terminated, do not attempt to evade the moderation action by creating or using another account when doing so violates a restriction placed on your account.

15. Keep Comments Appropriate

Comments are part of the Ship and must follow the same basic rules as uploaded Verses.

  • No harassment.
  • No doxxing.
  • No threats.
  • No spam.
  • No scams.
  • No hateful abuse.
  • No prohibited sexual content.

16. Moderation

ClarkVerse may use simple moderation systems and moderation review to detect violations.

Moderation actions can include:

  • Content removal
  • Warnings
  • Feature restrictions
  • Temporary bans
  • Longer suspensions
  • Permanent termination

17. Serious Violations

Some violations can result in immediate termination instead of a temporary ban.

Examples may include serious doxxing, credible threats, severe exploitation, major security attacks, or other extremely serious violations of the rules.

18. Moderation Isn't Perfect

ClarkVerse's moderation systems may make mistakes.

If an appeal or support process is available, users may use it to request that a moderation action be reviewed.

19. Help Keep the Ship Safe

If you see content that violates these rules, use the available reporting tools rather than engaging with the violation.

Be respectful, protect other people's privacy, and help make ClarkVerse a fun place for everyone.

20. Agreement

By using ClarkVerse, you agree to follow these rules, the ClarkVerse Terms & Conditions, and the ClarkVerse Privacy Policy.

Rules may be updated as ClarkVerse grows. Users may be required to review updated rules before continuing to use the platform.
